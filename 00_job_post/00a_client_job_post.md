Summary
We are seeking a Data Analyst to help a growing direct-to-consumer skincare brand, make sense of two years of Shopify sales data. Recommend what to prioritise over the next 6 months.
Build a Power BI dashboard we can use going forward, not a one-off report.
Must-have skills:
Comfortable working with a Shopify CSV export and a product data file.
Strong Power BI experience - building living dashboards, not static reports.
Clear communicator able to explain insights to a non-technical founder.
Bonus: experience with small DTC or e-commerce brands.
